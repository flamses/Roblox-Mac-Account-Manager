# MacBlox v1.1.2 🔧

- shows Roblox's account-moderation restriction instead of a misleading generic 403 login error
- checks the account session before preparing a client, so blocked accounts fail quickly
- distinguishes expired logins from moderation and rate limits
- checks that a saved login belongs to the account being launched

MacBlox cannot remove Roblox moderation restrictions. Sign in on Roblox to review the notice or appeal through Roblox. Select another unrestricted account to keep playing.

Download **MacBlox-v1.1.2-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip** below. macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon. Quit MacBlox before replacing it; saved accounts stay in Keychain.
